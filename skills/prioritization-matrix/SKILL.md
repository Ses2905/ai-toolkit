# Prioritization Matrix

## When to use
Use this to cut a crowded option list down fast. It picks the two dimensions that actually drive the choice (such as impact and feasibility), scores each option, plots them on a 2x2, and reads off a prioritized shortlist with a clear rationale.

## Instructions
1. Choose the two dimensions that most discriminate between options (e.g. impact and feasibility).
2. Define a clear scoring scale for each axis so placement is consistent.
3. Score every option on both axes with a one-line justification.
4. Plot the options on the two-by-two and identify the high-priority quadrant.
5. Read off a prioritized shortlist and explain why the bottom options drop out.

## Example prompts
- "Plot these initiatives on an impact-versus-effort 2x2 and tell me what to prioritize."
- "Score and rank my options on the two dimensions that matter most for this decision."

## Output
A scored option table plus a 2x2 placement and a ranked shortlist with rationale.
