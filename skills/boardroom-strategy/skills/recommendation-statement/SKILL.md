# Recommendation Statement

## When to use
Reach for this to land the answer. It writes one unambiguous recommendation, the few reasons it is right, the precise ask of the board, and the conditions or confidence level, so the deck makes a decision instead of presenting options forever.

## Instructions
1. Write the recommendation as one declarative sentence naming what to do and by when.
2. State the two or three reasons it is the right choice, tied back to the evidence.
3. Specify the exact ask: the approval, budget, or resource the board must grant.
4. Name the conditions under which the recommendation holds and your confidence level.
5. Pressure-test that a reader could approve or reject on this alone, with no ambiguity.

## Example prompts
- "Write a single committed recommendation with rationale and a precise board ask."
- "Turn my analysis into one clear recommendation, the ask, and the conditions it depends on."

## Output
A one-sentence recommendation followed by rationale bullets, a defined ask, and stated conditions and confidence.
