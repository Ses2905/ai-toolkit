# Objection And QA Prep

## When to use
Reach for this right before the meeting. It generates the objections each stakeholder is likely to raise, drafts tight answers with evidence, decides which to address proactively in slides versus hold for Q&A, and lists the backup material to have ready.

## Instructions
1. List the hardest questions and objections each key stakeholder is likely to raise.
2. Decide which objections would block the decision and must be pre-empted in the main deck.
3. Draft a crisp, specific answer to each, backed by evidence or a clear "we will follow up."
4. Hold detail-level or verification questions for live Q&A with backup slides ready.
5. Prepare a short bridge for unknowns so the presenter stays calm and credible.

## Example prompts
- "Anticipate the board's toughest objections to this strategy and draft crisp answers."
- "Help me decide which questions to pre-empt in the deck and which to hold for Q&A."

## Output
A question-and-answer list tagged pre-empt or Q&A, with evidence notes and a backup-material checklist.
