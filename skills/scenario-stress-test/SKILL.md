# Scenario Stress Test

## When to use
Reach for this when the recommendation depends on assumptions that might not hold. It defines a few distinct plausible futures along the most uncertain, high-impact drivers, scores each option against them, and surfaces which choice is robust rather than merely optimal on paper.

## Instructions
1. Identify the two drivers that are both most uncertain and most impactful.
2. Combine them into three or four distinct, plausible future scenarios.
3. Score how each option performs under each scenario.
4. Identify options that perform acceptably across the widest range of scenarios.
5. Flag the fragile options that only win in one future and the triggers that would signal each scenario arriving.

## Example prompts
- "Stress-test my strategic options across a few plausible future scenarios."
- "Which option is most robust if the market goes one of several ways? Build the scenarios."

## Output
A scenario-by-option matrix with performance scores, a robustness ranking, and scenario trigger signals.
