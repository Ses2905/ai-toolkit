# Action Title Writing

## When to use
Reach for this to replace label headings ("Revenue Overview") with takeaways ("Revenue grew despite a shrinking market because of pricing"). It writes a complete-sentence action title for every slide and checks that, read together, they form the narrative spine.

## Instructions
1. For each slide, state the single message it must deliver in one full sentence.
2. Rewrite it as an assertive action title that gives the takeaway, not the topic.
3. Strip vague label headings and any title that is obviously true or non-insightful.
4. Read all titles top to bottom and confirm they form one coherent argument.
5. Tighten each title to fit one or two lines and lead with the so-what.

## Example prompts
- "Write action titles for these slides so the headlines alone tell the story."
- "Turn my label headings into assertive takeaway titles that read as a narrative spine."

## Output
A per-slide list of action titles plus a top-to-bottom spine read-through and an insight check.
