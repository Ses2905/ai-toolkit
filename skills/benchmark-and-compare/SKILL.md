# Benchmark And Compare

## When to use
Use this when a number means nothing in isolation. It selects a fair comparison set, normalizes the data, and shows where the organization stands relative to it, surfacing the gaps and outliers that justify or challenge the strategy.

## Instructions
1. Choose a comparison set that is genuinely like-for-like and state why it is fair.
2. Normalize the metrics so the comparison is apples-to-apples.
3. Plot the organization's position against the benchmark set.
4. Identify the largest leads, lags, and outliers and what each implies.
5. Translate the comparison into one clear so-what about competitive position.

## Example prompts
- "Benchmark our performance against peers and tell me where we lag the most."
- "Build a comparison showing our position versus best-in-class and the so-what."

## Output
A benchmark table or ranked comparison with the organization flagged and a one-line positional so-what.
